Case study 01

POS / F&B Transaction Flow

Focus: transaction correctness
Fokus: ketepatan transaksi

Test objective

Tujuan pengujian

Validate key point-of-sale flows from item selection to purchase completion.

Memvalidasi alur utama POS dari pemilihan item hingga transaksi selesai.

Coverage to consider

Cakupan yang dipertimbangkan

Required fields, price/total calculation, payment outcome, error handling, and transaction-state consistency.

Field wajib, perhitungan harga/total, hasil pembayaran, penanganan error, dan konsistensi status transaksi.

Case study 02

Loyalty / Voucher Rules

Focus: business-rule validation
Fokus: validasi aturan bisnis

Test objective

Tujuan pengujian

Check that loyalty and voucher behavior follows configured business rules.

Memeriksa apakah perilaku loyalty dan voucher sesuai aturan bisnis yang dikonfigurasi.

Coverage to consider

Cakupan yang dipertimbangkan

Eligibility, validity period, quota boundary, repeat redemption, user state, and API/UI consistency.

Kelayakan, masa berlaku, batas kuota, redeem berulang, status pengguna, dan konsistensi API/UI.

Case study 03

Reservation Management

Focus: customer + back-office flows
Fokus: alur pelanggan + back-office

Test objective

Tujuan pengujian

Validate customer reservation journeys and back-office management behavior.

Memvalidasi alur reservasi pelanggan dan perilaku pengelolaan back-office.

Coverage to consider

Cakupan yang dipertimbangkan

Reservation type, date/time, seat availability where applicable, event vs regular booking, and status updates.

Jenis reservasi, tanggal/waktu, ketersediaan kursi bila berlaku, pemesanan event vs reguler, dan pembaruan status.

Case study 04

HRIS / Petty Cash Workflow

Focus: permissions and data integrity
Fokus: hak akses dan integritas data

Test objective

Tujuan pengujian

Check internal workflow rules and data integrity across user roles.

Memeriksa aturan workflow internal dan integritas data pada berbagai peran pengguna.

Coverage to consider

Cakupan yang dipertimbangkan

Role permissions, approval flow, mandatory fields, valid/invalid inputs, and status transitions.

Hak akses peran, alur persetujuan, field wajib, input valid/tidak valid, dan transisi status.

06 / WorkflowAlur kerja

From requirements
to release insight.

Dari requirement
hingga insight rilis.

A repeatable approach to keep testing focused and findings actionable.

Pendekatan berulang agar pengujian tetap fokus dan temuan dapat ditindaklanjuti.

STEP 01

Understand

Pahami

Review requirements, acceptance criteria, designs, and dependencies.

Tinjau requirement, acceptance criteria, desain, dan dependensi.

STEP 02

Plan & design

Rencanakan

Identify risk, coverage, test data, and positive/negative scenarios.

Identifikasi risiko, cakupan, data uji, dan skenario positif/negatif.

STEP 03

Execute

Eksekusi

Run tests and compare actual behavior with expected results.

Jalankan tes dan bandingkan perilaku aktual dengan hasil yang diharapkan.

STEP 04

Report

Laporkan

Document reproducible defects with evidence and impact.

Dokumentasikan defect yang dapat direproduksi beserta bukti dan dampaknya.

STEP 05

Retest & regress

Retest & regresi

Verify fixes and check related areas for side effects.

Verifikasi perbaikan dan periksa area terkait untuk efek samping.

STEP 06

Communicate

Komunikasikan

Summarize coverage, open risks, and quality observations.

Rangkum cakupan, risiko terbuka, dan temuan kualitas.
